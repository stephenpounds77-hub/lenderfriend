Processing for Florida originators

Third-party loan processing built around your pipeline

Florida is a big, spread-out state to originate in. A pipeline might include a purchase in Jacksonville, a refinance in Orlando, a condo on the Tampa Bay side, and a file in Miami-Dade, all in the same week. Every one of those files needs the same thing: documents collected, conditions tracked, and a clean package ready for underwriting.

Lender Friend is based in Palm Coast, in Flagler County on Florida’s Atlantic coast between Jacksonville and Daytona Beach. Florida is our home state, and we built our processing service for originators here who want to spend their time with borrowers and referral partners instead of chasing paperwork.

We work as an extension of your team. You keep the client relationship and the origination; we take on the processing workload and keep each file organized from application to clear-to-close.
