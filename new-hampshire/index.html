Processing for New Hampshire originators

Third-party loan processing built around your pipeline

New Hampshire is a smaller state, and many originators here run lean operations where every hour spent on processing is an hour not spent with borrowers. Files may come from Manchester and Nashua in the south, Concord in the capital region, Portsmouth on the Seacoast, or the Lakes Region and North Country.

Lender Friend provides third-party mortgage processing for New Hampshire loan officers, brokers, and lenders. We collect documents, manage conditions, organize each file, and prepare it for underwriting, so a small team can handle a bigger pipeline without hiring in-house processing staff.

We work remotely and inside your existing systems. You keep the client relationship; we keep the file moving.

Many New Hampshire loan officers serve borrowers who are buying in one part of the state while working in another, or relocating from neighboring states. That makes clear communication and a well-documented file especially important. We focus on thorough documentation and proactive condition management so files reach underwriting complete, with fewer surprises along the way, whether the property is in Nashua, Concord, Portsmouth, or up north.
