Nature of our services

Lender Friend LLC provides third-party mortgage loan processing services to mortgage originators, brokers, and lenders, under the state licenses listed above. Our processing fee is a fixed fee per file and is borrower-paid and non-shoppable. Lender Friend LLC does not originate loans, make credit decisions, or issue loan approvals.
