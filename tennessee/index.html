Processing for Tennessee originators

Third-party loan processing built around your pipeline

Tennessee stretches about 400 miles from the Mississippi River to the Appalachians, and originators here often work files in more than one of its three Grand Divisions. A loan officer in Nashville may have files in Memphis, Knoxville, or Chattanooga in the same pipeline.

Lender Friend provides third-party mortgage processing for Tennessee loan officers, brokers, and lenders. We handle the documents, the conditions, and the file setup so you can focus on originating, and on the borrowers and agents who send you business.

Our processors work remotely and fit into the systems you already use, so it doesn’t matter which part of the state you or your borrowers are in.

The originators we work with in Tennessee range from individual brokers to growing teams. Some want help through a busy stretch; others want to stop managing processing in-house altogether. A Memphis broker, a Knoxville loan officer, and a Nashville team all get the same thing from us: organized files, proactive condition management, and a fixed fee per file that is easy to plan around and easy to explain to borrowers.
