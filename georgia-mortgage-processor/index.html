Processing for Georgia originators

Third-party loan processing built around your pipeline

Most Georgia pipelines run through metro Atlanta, but plenty of originators also work files in Savannah on the coast, Augusta along the South Carolina line, and communities like Macon, Columbus, and Athens. Different markets, same need: complete, well-organized files that are ready for underwriting.

Lender Friend provides third-party mortgage processing for Georgia loan officers, brokers, and lenders. We collect and review documents, manage conditions, organize the file, and prepare it for underwriting so you can focus on production and your client relationships.

We’re based next door in Palm Coast, Florida, and we work remotely inside your existing workflow. Your borrowers keep working with you; we handle the processing load.

Georgia originators come to us for a few common reasons: a busy Atlanta purchase season that outgrows an in-house processor, a branch in Savannah or Augusta that needs support without adding headcount, or a broker who simply wants a predictable cost per file. In each case, the goal is the same — cleaner files, fewer underwriting conditions, and faster turn times, so loan officers can get back to originating.
