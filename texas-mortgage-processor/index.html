Processing for Texas originators

Third-party loan processing built around your pipeline

Texas originators cover a lot of ground. Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, Austin, and San Antonio are each major markets on their own, and many loan officers work files across several of them plus the smaller cities in between. Keeping every file organized while you keep producing is where a processing partner earns its keep.

Lender Friend provides third-party mortgage processing for Texas loan officers, brokers, and lenders. We handle document collection, condition follow-up, file organization, and underwriting preparation so you can stay focused on borrowers, builders, and real estate partners.

We plug into the systems and processes you already use. You stay the point of contact for your client; we keep the file complete and moving behind the scenes.
