Mortgage Processing FAQ

What does a third-party mortgage processor do?

We collect and review borrower documents, manage conditions, organize the file, and prepare it for underwriting so originators can focus on production and client relationships.

Who does Lender Friend work with?

Mortgage originators, brokers, and lenders. We take document collection, condition clearing, file organization, and underwriting prep off your desk so you can focus on originating.

Is your service shoppable by the borrower?

No. We are a third-party, non-shoppable, borrower-paid processing service.

What loan types do you process?

We support conventional, FHA, VA, DSCR, and other common residential mortgage products.

Which states are you licensed in?

Florida, Georgia, New Hampshire, Tennessee, and Texas. Lender Friend LLC, NMLS 2556222 — see our Licensing page for each state's license number.

How does pricing work?

We charge one simple fixed fee per file, so costs are predictable and easy to build into your Loan Estimates. The fee is borrower-paid and non-shoppable.

What systems do you work in?

We work in Arive and LendingPad, and we adapt to your workflow and preferred systems.

Does Lender Friend originate loans or approve them?

No. We provide third-party processing services only — we do not originate loans, make credit decisions, or issue loan approvals.

How do I get started?

Call Stephen Pounds at 386-225-3503 or email onboarding@thelenderfriend.com. We talk through your pipeline and systems, integrate with your workflow, and start handling your files.

Is there an offer for new clients?

Half off the processing fee on your first loan file. New clients only. Not a loan rate or approval offer. NMLS 2556222.
