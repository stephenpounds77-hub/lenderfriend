Who we are

This website, www.thelenderfriend.com, is operated by Lender Friend LLC, a third-party mortgage processing company located in Palm Coast, Florida (NMLS 2556222). In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" mean Lender Friend LLC.

Information we collect

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect the information you choose to provide: your name, company, email address, phone number, and message. If you call or email us, we receive the information you share in that conversation.

Information collected automatically

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit in order to operate and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement for details on how it handles this data.

How we use information

We use the information you send us to respond to your inquiry, discuss our processing services, and communicate with you about a business relationship.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run this website and our business, such as GitHub Pages for website hosting and FormSubmit for delivering contact-form messages, or when required by law.

Borrower information

This website is intended for mortgage originators, brokers, and lenders. Please do not submit borrower Social Security numbers, account numbers, or other sensitive borrower data through the website contact form.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes described or as required by law (mortgage records may be subject to legal retention requirements). We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you submitted by emailing onboarding@thelenderfriend.com or by reaching us through our Contact page.
